Drag a knob up/down (or scroll, or use arrow keys) to turn it. Double-click to reset. Bass Boost shelves up the low end; Reverb blends in a convolved room decay; Filter sweeps a low-pass to the left of center and a high-pass to the right; Distortion drives a soft-clip waveshaper; Bitcrush crushes bit depth and sample rate for a lo-fi/glitch sound; Echo repeats the signal with decaying slap-back delays; Tremolo pulses the volume rhythmically. All run through the same Web Audio graph as the equalizer.
